### DESCRIPTION OF PARCELS
- **Parcel 1:** Cloth parcel bearing wax seal intact. Contained country-made 9mm pistol (Marked **W/1**, physical serial WB-8819).
- **Parcel 2:** Sealed plastic vial containing 1 fired 9mm cartridge casing (Marked **C/1**, headstamp KF-9mm).

### LABORATORY EXAMINATION & FINDINGS
1. Country pistol **W/1** is in working order. Test rounds successfully chambered and test fired in ballistic water tank.
2. Comparison macroscope examination (Leica FSC at 25x magnification) reveals:
   - The firing pin indentation on the primer cup of fired casing **C/1** exhibits identical rectangular contour and shear marks as test fired casings from **W/1**.
   - Breech face striation signatures on the head of **C/1** match the unique machining ridges of breech block **W/1**.

### CONCLUSION
Fired cartridge casing **C/1** has been discharged from country-made pistol **W/1** (WB-8819) to the exclusion of all other firearms.
